01About us

A good event is more than a programme — it is the atmosphere, the people and a night you want to remember.

Henrix Band plays live music at weddings, private parties, birthdays, corporate events, year-end celebrations and other special occasions.

Our goal is simple — to create a real party. We play well-known Latvian and international songs that people sing along and dance to, and that work for all kinds of audiences.

06FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to book Henrix Band?

The price depends on the line-up and the event format. Send us the date, venue, approximate number of guests and the planned format of the evening, and we will prepare an exact quote.

How long is the music programme?

The standard programme is 3 sets of 50 minutes. Depending on the event schedule, it can also be split into 4 shorter sets around dinner, the host’s programme, awards, performances or a DJ.

Which line-up should we choose?

Henrix Essential (3 musicians) is a compact option for wedding dinners, birthdays and smaller events. Henrix Live+ (4 musicians) is the most versatile line-up for weddings, parties and corporate events. Henrix Full Experience (5 musicians) is the full concert-party experience for large weddings, balls and celebrations with more guests.

Do you play outside Riga?

Yes, Henrix Band plays at events all over Latvia. Transport within Riga is included in the quote; travel costs may be added for events outside Riga.

What is included?

The band in the chosen line-up, a 3 × 50 minute programme adapted to the event schedule, the band’s instruments and technical equipment, sound for the agreed format, transport within Riga, technical setup and soundcheck. Travel costs may be added outside Riga.

What songs do you play?

Our repertoire has more than 70 well-known songs in Latvian, English, Spanish and Portuguese — from sing-along favourites to high-energy dance-floor hits. We adapt the repertoire to the event and the guests; the full list is available on request.

How do we book the band?

Fill in the enquiry form on this page, write to henrixband@gmail.com or call +371 25 972 689. We reply in English or Latvian.
